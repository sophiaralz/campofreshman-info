Advice from graduated Class of 2024

Education Social Life
"Don't fall behind in any classes!" "Don't be afraid to speak to new people and make new friends. You'll become more outgoing that way."
"Try not to stress too much about grades. Academics are important, but so is having fun!" "Make new friends- just cause you have the old ones doesn't mean they're going to be the best ones."
"Have an open mind, take advantage of every opportunity, and be kind." "Go to as many sports games as possible and recover after practice."
"Make sure to stay organized and make a calendar and to-do list system for yourself early on!" "Participate in senior assassins if you have the chance!"
"Pick classes that actually interest you." "Take it easy - high school is a marathon, not a sprint. Also, you won't get everything in one go - and that's okay."

Studying tips

"Don't procrastinate."
"DO NOT CRAM. Start studying at least 3 days in advance."
"Do homework on the day it was assigned. It really frees up time and lightens the stress."
"Repetition! Review class assignments AND the study guide -> the tests are usually what was talked about in class!"
"Read your notes out loud."
"Take small breaks in between to keep your mind engaged and so you don't feel burnt out staring at pages or screens for hours at a time. This does not mean go on social media and never coming back!"
"Use Quizlet and all the resources your teacher provides!!"
"Write stuff down, it helps with memorization, especially dates."
"Take your academy time seriously. Attend your teacher's academies when you need to ask questions or just study."
"Pop in both headphones and go to the library for a deep study session."
"Put your phone in a different room so it's not a distraction.
