Kristi Davis Algebra 1 and Geometry Ms. Davis' favorite hobby is reading. The most memorable moment she has had at Campo so far was the first rally of the 2023-2024 school year, where she felt the leadership team did an outstanding job. Her favorite aspect of teaching is transforming students' attitudes toward math; many students who initially dislike math end up enjoying it after being in her class. She appreciates Campo for its friendly community and well-maintained campus, thanks to the considerate behavior of the students. Ms. Davis has a student at Las Lomas and two children who have graduated from there. This dual perspective as both a teacher and a parent provides her with valuable insights into the experiences and challenges Campo students face, as well as what lies ahead for them.
Jonathan Clark Living Earth Mr. Clark's favorite hobby is a combination of outdoor activities such as rafting, camping, and gardening. The most memorable moments he has had at Campo so far are the rallies. His favorite part about teaching is the opportunity to spend time with such splendid people. He enjoys Campo because of the wonderful community.
Kate Guy Living Earth and Juman Anatomy & Psychology [for sophomores, juniors, seniors] Ms. Guy's favorite hobby is camping with her family. The most memorable moment she has had at Campo was hosting Campo Night Live [CNL], which was nerve-wracking but incredibly fun. Her favorite part about teaching is getting to know and support her students. She loves sharing her passion for science and sparking her students' interest in the subject. Ms. Guy believes Campo is the best school she has ever worked at, praising the hardworking and kind students and the supportive and kind staff. She appreciates the strong school spirit and how much everyone cares about Campo, creating a great vibe. Her advice for incoming freshmen is to use their academy time wisely. Many freshmen waste this valuable time on their phones or computers, but by visiting teachers for help, studying, or completing work, they can accomplish a lot and reduce their workload at home.
Raymond Meadows World History Mr. Meadows has a passion for mountain biking, which he considers his favorite hobby. One of his most memorable moments at Campo was when the school won the Max Preps Athletic School of the Year for California in 2019-2020. He loves teaching because it allows him to work with and get to know the students. Mr. Meadows appreciates Campo for its supportive community, including the staff, teachers, parents, and administration. He wishes the freshmen the best of luck in their first year at Campo and encourages them to enjoy their high school experience to the fullest!
